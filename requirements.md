# Local Body Candidate Application — 9-Step Requirements & Feature Specifications

This document outlines the detailed specifications for the 9 key features and requirements requested for the BJP Local Body Candidate Application flow.

---

### Step 1: Photo Upload
* **Purpose**: Capture the applicant's official portrait for identification and card generation.
* **Details**: 
  * Allows applicants to upload a clear passport-style photograph.
  * Supports image cropping / preview before final submission.
  * The uploaded photo is embedded into the candidate's post-submission ID card.

---

### Step 2: Free Text — [Sample Content]
* **Purpose**: Provide space for candidates to describe their personal background and achievements.
* **Details**: 
  * Includes a multi-line text area with helpful sample placeholder text.
  * Guides applicants on what details to include (e.g., social service history, party involvement, community work).

---

### Step 3: Video Upload
* **Purpose**: Allow candidates to submit a video message or self-introduction.
* **Details**: 
  * Supports direct video file upload or video link submission (e.g., YouTube / Google Drive / MP4).
  * Enables organizers to visually evaluate candidate communication and presentation skills.

---

### Step 4: What Strategy You Have to Win This Ward?
* **Purpose**: Evaluate the candidate's ground strategy and electoral viability.
* **Details**: 
  * Dedicated open-ended essay prompt focused on ward-level strategy.
  * Captures key local issues identified by the candidate, target demographics, and action plan to win the ward.

---

### Step 5: Candidate Card After Application Submission
* **Purpose**: Provide immediate visual confirmation and a downloadable summary card.
* **Details**: 
  * Automatically generates a digital Candidate Card upon successful application submission.
  * Displays Candidate Photo, Name, Application ID, Ward/Local Body name, and a unique QR code for verification.

---

### Step 6: BJP Membership Link (If Not a Member) — Optional Click & Proceed
* **Purpose**: Facilitate BJP membership registration for new applicants.
* **Details**: 
  * Displays an optional prompt & link for applicants who do not yet possess a BJP Membership ID.
  * Allows them to click, register on the official BJP portal, get their ID, and return to complete their candidate application.

---

### Step 7: Space for 2 Additional Questions
* **Purpose**: Ensure flexibility for organizers to collect extra candidate details.
* **Details**: 
  * Reserves 2 flexible form slots in the backend schema and frontend UI.
  * Allows organizers to dynamically configure 2 custom questions (e.g., previous election experience, availability).

---

### Step 8: Profile [Optional]
* **Purpose**: Allow candidates to share supplementary background information.
* **Details**: 
  * Optional section for candidates to attach external profiles, resume/CV links, or social media handles.

---

### Step 9: Post-Submission Edit Policy
* **Purpose**: Ensure data integrity after submission.
* **Details**: 
  * Once the application is submitted, direct editing by the applicant is locked.
  * Clear instructions are displayed advising the applicant: *"If you need to make changes to your application, please get in touch directly with the campaign organiser."*
